## A7 — Temporal Evidence Ordering Axiom

> **"Evidence must be ordered by time of occurrence, not time of discovery or submission."**

A7 is the first ordering gate in the WEIGHTS phase.
It prevents institutional narratives from re-sequencing events
to obscure causation — the most common forensic distortion.
No causal map is valid unless its chronology is anchored to occurrence time.
